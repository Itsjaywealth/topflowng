Introducing BizFlow — Invoicing & Business Tools for Nigerian SMEs

2 min read · August 2026

We're excited to announce BizFlow — a complete business management suite built right into TopFlowNG. Now you can send invoices, manage clients, run payroll, and get paid — all without leaving your account.

What You Can Do

Invoicing. Create professional invoices with line items, VAT, and due dates. Send them by email with a "Pay Now" button — your clients pay securely and the payment is recorded for you automatically.

CRM. Keep track of your clients, their contact details, and invoice history. Everything in one place.

Payroll. Run monthly payroll for your staff. Track who's been paid and who's due.

HR. Manage your team — staff records, roles, salaries, and start dates. Buy airtime or data for staff directly from the HR page.

How It Works

BizFlow is at bizflow.html in your TopFlowNG account. It uses the same login — no separate sign-up needed. Your data is synced to the cloud automatically, so you never lose it.

Client Payments

When you send an invoice, your client receives an email with a "Pay Now" button. They click it, pay securely (powered by TopFlowNG), and the invoice is marked paid automatically. All payment volume flows through TopFlowNG's payment infrastructure.
