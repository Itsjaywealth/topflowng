How to Send Airtime to Any Network in Nigeria

3 min read · Updated August 2026

Need to send airtime to a friend, family member, or staff? TopFlowNG makes it easy to send airtime to any Nigerian network — MTN, Glo, Airtel, and T2 (9mobile) — instantly from your wallet.

Step-by-Step Guide

1. Log into your TopFlowNG account.

2. Tap "Airtime" from the services menu.

3. Select the recipient's network (MTN, Glo, Airtel, or T2).

4. Enter the phone number you want to top up.

5. Enter the amount (minimum ₦50).

6. Confirm with your transaction PIN.

7. The airtime is sent instantly — you'll get a confirmation and receipt.

Save Beneficiaries for Faster Top-Ups

You can save frequently topped-up numbers as beneficiaries. This way you don't need to re-enter the phone number every time. Just select the saved contact and enter the amount.

Schedule Recurring Airtime

If you send airtime to the same person regularly (like a staff member or family member), you can set up a scheduled purchase. The airtime will be sent automatically on your chosen schedule — daily, weekly, or monthly.
