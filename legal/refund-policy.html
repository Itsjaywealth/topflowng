Refund & Reversal Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains how refunds and reversals work on TopFlowNG. We aim to handle every transaction fairly and transparently. Because most purchases are fulfilled by integrated third-party providers, whether a refund is possible depends on the state of the order.

1. Failed Transactions

If a transaction fails — the provider does not confirm fulfilment — the amount is reversed back to your TopFlowNG wallet. In most cases this happens automatically. If your wallet has not been credited after a failed transaction, email hello@topflowng.com with the transaction reference and we will investigate.

2. Pending Transactions

Pending transactions are awaiting confirmation from the provider. A pending order is not a refund case — it is still being processed. Please wait for it to resolve or use the status check on the transaction detail page. Do not place a duplicate purchase while an order is pending. If a pending order resolves as failed, the amount is reversed to your wallet.

3. Successfully Fulfilled Purchases

Once a provider confirms fulfilment (for example, airtime or data delivered, an electricity token issued, or a cable TV subscription activated), the purchase is complete and generally not refundable, because the value has already been delivered to the recipient.

4. Wallet Reversals

Where TopFlowNG or our providers identify an error affecting your order — such as a failed fulfilment, a provider error or an amount mismatch — the appropriate amount is reversed to your TopFlowNG wallet.

5. Duplicate Charges

If you are charged more than once for the same order and we or our providers confirm the duplication, the duplicate amount is reversed to your wallet. To help us resolve it quickly, include the transaction references of the affected orders.

6. Provider Disputes

Some cases require the service provider to confirm what happened on their side. Resolution time can vary depending on the provider's response. We will keep you informed and reverse your wallet as soon as we can confirm the appropriate outcome.

7. How to Request Help

Email hello@topflowng.com and include your TopFlowNG transaction reference. Our team will investigate and respond.

8. No Guarantee of Provider Refunds

TopFlowNG processes refunds to your TopFlowNG wallet for failed or erroneous orders. We cannot guarantee that external providers will refund value they have already delivered, and we do not promise refunds in scenarios outside our control.

TopFlowNG support will never ask for your password or transaction PIN.
