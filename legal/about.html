About TopFlowNG

Airtime, data, electricity, cable TV and exam pins — paid from one simple Naira wallet.

TopFlowNG is a Nigerian platform that lets you handle everyday digital and utility payments in one place. Fund your wallet once, then buy what you need in a few taps.

What you can do

  • Airtime — top up any Nigerian network.
  • Data bundles — buy data plans across major networks.
  • Electricity — pay for electricity tokens.
  • Cable TV — renew cable TV subscriptions.
  • Education — purchase exam-related pins such as result checker pins.
  • Wallet — fund once, track every transaction, and keep records in your history.
  • Scheduled purchases — set up recurring or scheduled purchases where available.

How it works

Create an account, fund your TopFlowNG wallet through our integrated payment processor, set a transaction PIN to protect purchases, then place your order. Each purchase appears in your history with a reference, receipt and status.
