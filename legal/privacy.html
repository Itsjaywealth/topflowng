Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TopFlowNG ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the TopFlowNG platform at topflowng.com. By using the platform you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number and password (stored securely as a hash) when you register.
  • Transaction data — the services you buy, amounts, recipient identifiers (phone number, meter number, smart card number), references and transaction status.
  • Payment information — when you fund your wallet, payment processing is handled by an integrated third-party payment processor. We do not store your card details.
  • Technical logs — IP address, browser/device information and standard server logs for security and reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate and secure the services you use.
  • To process purchases through integrated service providers.
  • To send transactional emails, such as purchase confirmations and password reset links.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse or security issues.
  • To improve reliability and support quality.

3. Service Providers & Processors

We work with third-party providers to deliver services and process payments. These providers may receive the information needed to fulfil your request, such as your recipient identifier or payment amount. Examples include service/utility providers, payment processors and email delivery services. They process data under their own policies and only for the purposes of the services they provide to us.

4. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including secure connections (TLS), password hashing, transaction PIN protection, access controls and rate limiting. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and transaction records to operate the platform, support customers and meet our obligations. Financial and transaction records are typically retained in line with accounting and fraud-prevention requirements. You may request details of what we hold by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion where it does not conflict with financial record-keeping requirements. To make a request, email hello@topflowng.com.

7. Cookies & Analytics

The platform does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Standard technical data needed to operate and secure the platform (for example, session security) may be processed as described above.

8. Children

TopFlowNG is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@topflowng.com.

TopFlowNG support will never ask for your password or transaction PIN.
