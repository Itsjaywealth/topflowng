FAQ

Common questions.

Still have questions? Email us at hello@topflowng.com

What services can I pay for on TopFlowNG?

You can buy airtime and data for MTN, Glo, Airtel and T2; pay electricity bills for all major distribution companies (IKEDC, EKEDC, AEDC, PHEDC, KEDC, IBEDC and more); subscribe to DStv, GOtv and StarTimes; and purchase WAEC result checker PINs.

How does a purchase work?

Create a free account, choose your service, enter the recipient details (phone number, meter number or smartcard), review the amount, confirm with your transaction PIN, and your service is delivered instantly — with a receipt in your history.

How long does delivery take?

Most purchases are processed and delivered within seconds. If a transaction stays pending for longer, do not repeat the purchase — use the "Check status" option or contact support at hello@topflowng.com.

What is a transaction PIN?

A transaction PIN is a 4–6 digit code you set up in your account settings. It is required to confirm every purchase and protects your account from unauthorised transactions.

What happens if a transaction fails or is pending?

A pending transaction means we are still confirming delivery with the provider. Do not buy the same service again while it is pending. If it does not resolve, contact us at hello@topflowng.com with your transaction reference and we will investigate.

Is my account secure?

Yes. Every purchase requires your transaction PIN. Your password is stored securely and TopFlowNG staff will never ask for your password or PIN. If you ever suspect unauthorised access, change your password immediately and contact support.

Who operates TopFlowNG?

TopFlowNG is operated by Brandverse Ventures. For any enquiry, email hello@topflowng.com.

How do I contact support?

Use the live chat icon on the site, or email hello@topflowng.com. Support is available during business hours. For urgent transaction issues, include your reference number for faster resolution.

Getting started
What is TopFlowNG?

TopFlowNG is a digital services platform for buying airtime, data, electricity, cable TV subscriptions and supported exam PINs — with a receipt and a clear history for everything.

How do I create an account?

Tap "Create free account", enter your full name, a valid phone number and email, then choose a password. You can start buying right away.

Payments
How do I pay for a service?

Choose a service, enter the recipient or account details, and pay securely for that order at checkout. Your payment is verified before your service is processed.

What happens after I pay?

Once your payment is confirmed, we process your order and the provider delivers your service. You can track the status in your transaction history.

Why do I need a transaction PIN?

Your 4–6 digit PIN protects every purchase. It is set once from your Account page and never stored in plain text.

Purchases
How quickly are purchases delivered?

Most airtime, data and bill payments are delivered within seconds. Some orders are held as "pending" while the provider confirms them — you are only charged once the order is confirmed.

What happens when a transaction is pending?

Pending transactions are being confirmed by the provider. You have not been charged. The order resolves automatically, and you can watch it in Transactions.

What happens if a purchase fails?

If a purchase fails, you are never charged. Payment is only captured once the provider confirms the order. The failed order appears in your transaction history.

Can I buy airtime for another phone number?

Yes. Enter any beneficiary phone number during checkout, or save frequent recipients in your Beneficiaries list for one-tap repeat purchases.

Electricity & cable TV
Where can I find an electricity token?

Your electricity token is shown immediately after a successful purchase and can be recovered any time from the transaction detail or the receipt.

Why did I receive more than one electricity token for one payment?

Some DISCOs split large vendings into multiple tokens for the meter. Each token is valid; enter them one at a time in any order.

Transactions & receipts
How do I view old transactions?

Open the Transactions tab from Home or the bottom navigation. Use the filters and search to find any purchase or top-up.

Where do I find my receipt?

Every completed purchase has a receipt. Open the transaction from your history and use Share, Download or Print to keep a copy.

Why is a transaction missing from my history?

History is fetched from your account's records in real time. Pull to refresh or reopen the Transactions tab. If it still does not appear, email hello@topflowng.com with the reference and we will investigate.

Account & security
How do I reset my password?

Tap "Forgot password" on the login screen, enter your registered phone number or email, and follow the reset link we send you.

I paid but my service has not been delivered — what now?

Open the transaction in your history to see its current status. If it is still processing, give it a few minutes. If it remains unresolved, email hello@topflowng.com with your TopFlowNG transaction reference and we will investigate.
