# TopFlowNG — First 100 Customers

## Objective
Reach and retain the first 100 paying customers on the wallet + VTU platform.

## Prerequisites (must be owner-decided before real transactions)
- **Markup/service fee** per product (not set — owner decision required).
- **Referral reward** amount (not set).
- **Live-certification authorization** + operator-owned low-value test targets (NOT authorized in this session).
- Real-money certification must pass before public traffic is encouraged (see final report).

## Activation plan
1. **Seed supply** — confirm VTPass wallet balance is healthy and top it up to cover expected first-week volume.
2. **Controlled live certification** — once owner authorizes, run low-value operator-owned purchases for airtime/data/electricity/cable; confirm delivery, tokens, receipts, reconciliation.
3. **Referral** — once reward value is set, launch the referral link in the dashboard.
4. **Support** — use the support@ email and transaction-help flow for the first cohort; resolve issues fast.
5. **Promotions** — only after owner sets discount values.

## Acquisition channels (owner to run/approve)
- Social (WhatsApp/Twitter/Instagram) with the share/referral link.
- Local communities (students, small businesses) — strong fit for airtime/data.
- Word of mouth via referrals.

## Retention mechanics (already built)
- Wallet + receipt + history + notification centre keep users informed.
- Scheduled purchases and beneficiaries reduce repeat friction.
- Transaction PIN builds trust.

## Success metrics
- 100 funded accounts.
- ≥ 90% purchase success rate.
- Reconciliation "In balance" every day.
- Referral conversion tracked.

## Guardrails
- Do not spend on ads without an owner-approved budget.
- Do not invent pricing/rewards.
